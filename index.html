About

Kiera Joy Krause lives in Spokane, Washington. She enjoys playing the piano, reading all genres of books in her spare time (because who can stick to just one genre?), and brainstorming new ideas for books to write.

She also loves being out in nature, rainy days, coffee, and listening to music. You can usually find her dinking around on a piano or hanging out with friends.
