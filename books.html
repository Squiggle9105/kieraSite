Scarred

Scarred when she was a child, Julianna thinks herself ugly, uncapable of loving and undeserving of love. She prides herself on being smart and logical, never allowing herself to succumb to emotion. So when her father loses his job, she does what she must and goes to work at Greenbriar Castle as a servant. What she thought would be a simple job turns out to be much more. Danger presses in on all sides, and she starts to question who her true allies are. With a rebelling kingdom taking other kingdoms captive, who can she really trust?

Assassin

Belladonna Nightshade has been a trained assassin since she was a little girl. Crowned the queen of Rowinth after her master's death, she lives in seclusion, hiding in her castle, her fears and anxiety keeping her from going practically anywhere. When her people have finally had enough, the newly-crowned queen flees her kingdom in terror, seeking refuge at her grandmother's house; the only family she has left. In her travels there, she meets the handsome and mysterious man Atlas, who seems to cause her to dwell on her past much more than she ever wished to, and appears to be hiding secrets of his own.
